Tenbō

Tower visibility puzzle on a 4×4 Latin square

How to play: Fill each cell with a tower height 1–4. No height repeats in any row or column (Latin square). Clue cells show an arrow and a number — looking from that cell in the arrow's direction, exactly that many towers are visible (taller towers block shorter ones behind). Click a cell to cycle heights. Clue and given cells are locked.
