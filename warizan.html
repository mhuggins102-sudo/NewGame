Warizan

KenKen / Calcudoku – fill the 4×4 grid with 1–4

Rules: Fill every cell with a number 1–4. Each row and column must contain each number exactly once (Latin square). Thick-bordered regions have an operator and target – the numbers in that region must produce the target using the operator. Click a cell, then press 1–4 or use the buttons below. Click a filled cell to clear it.
