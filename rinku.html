Rinku

Loop distance puzzle

Goal: Draw a single closed loop through the grid.
Controls: Click a cell to select it (orange border). Then click an adjacent cell to draw or remove a connection between them. The selection follows your clicks so you can keep drawing.
Rules: The loop must visit every numbered cell. Between consecutive same-colored numbers along the loop, the step count must equal their value. Each cell has 0 or 2 connections.
