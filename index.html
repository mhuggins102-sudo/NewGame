Puzzle Prototypes

Six math & spatial reasoning puzzle concepts — click to play each demo

🌉

Starbridge

Connect numbered dots with bridges. Each dot needs exactly N bridges touching it. Bridges cannot cross.

 🔲

Numawari

Divide the grid into rectangles. Each region contains one number equal to the rectangle's perimeter.

 🏙

Tenbō

Place towers of varying heights. Arrow clues tell you how many towers are visible looking in that direction.

 📦

Kagezumi

Stack blocks on a grid so the front and side shadow silhouettes match the given targets.

 🔗

Rinku

Draw a closed loop visiting all numbered cells. Distance between same-colored numbers must match their value.

 ✖

Warizan

Fill the grid 1-N (no row/col repeats). Each region's operator applied to its cells must hit the target number.
